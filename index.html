Portfolio · Healthcare AI

Chukki

An AI-driven medical decision support system for oncology — grounded in real-world evidence, clinical data, and actionable insights.

Chukki helps clinicians and care teams navigate complex oncology decisions with clarity. By unifying real-world evidence, longitudinal patient data, and intelligent analytics, it surfaces the insights that matter — treatment patterns, outcomes signals, and evidence-backed recommendations — so care teams can act with greater confidence at every stage of the cancer journey.

  • Oncology focus Purpose-built for cancer care pathways, from diagnosis through treatment selection and follow-up.
  • Real-world evidence Turns population-level outcomes and practice patterns into decision-ready clinical context.
  • Data & insights Transforms fragmented healthcare data into clear, trustworthy signals for care and research teams.
