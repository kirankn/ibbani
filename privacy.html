Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2025

Last Updated: September 12, 2025

At Ibbani, Inc. (“Ibbani,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), we are more than just a venture studio. We are a hub of innovation, driven by the pursuit of transformative ideas and dedicated to bringing them to life.

We envision a future where groundbreaking ideas seamlessly transition from concept to scalable businesses. By fostering a collaborative ecosystem that blends entrepreneurial drive with strategic guidance, we aim to create lasting ventures that drive societal change and economic impact.

Our approach is built on three pillars:

  • Ideation: We spark new ventures by helping transform creative ideas into executable business models.
  • Incubation: We provide a fertile ground for startups to grow through strategic partnerships and hands-on support.
  • Acceleration: We focus on scaling and sustainable growth by offering mentorship, market access, and operational excellence.

While our mission is bold, our commitment to privacy is simple: we respect and protect the information of all who interact with us.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.

We may collect limited non-personal information automatically:

  • Technical Data: Such as browser type, IP address, operating system, and general usage statistics.
  • Cookies & Analytics: We may use cookies or analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand site usage and improve functionality.

2. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used only to:

  • Improve the functionality, security, and performance of our website.
  • Monitor usage patterns to guide innovation and engagement.
  • Support our venture studio ecosystem and its processes (ideation, incubation, and acceleration).

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade information. Non-personal information may be shared only with:

  • Service Providers: Vendors who provide hosting, analytics, or technical support.
  • Legal Requirements: When required to comply with applicable laws or regulations.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard practices to safeguard our systems and prevent unauthorized access. Because we do not collect personal data, your exposure is minimal.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
